# FlowGuard V4 — Jury-Impact Creative Direction

Updated: 2026-10-27 (Asia/Jakarta)

## North star

The film should feel like a premium product launch crossed with a forensic engineering thriller: emotional stakes first, mechanisms made spatial, authentic proof treated as the hero, and a clean brand resolve. The desired reaction is not merely “many effects”; it is “every movement makes the product feel inevitable and credible.”

## Five motion principles

1. **Cause has direction.** Requests travel, fork, collide, reserve, and converge. Technical logic is choreographed spatially rather than explained by static cards.
2. **Proof gets the brightest stage.** Authentic screenshots enter with camera depth and controlled light, then settle into an undistorted reading hold.
3. **State changes create impact.** Failure, reset, pass, report, and merge events receive short flashes, pulse rings, tonal changes, and sound beats.
4. **The interface feels alive.** Moving sheens, cursor clicks, scan texture, timecode, scene telemetry, and progress markers create an interactive system-film language without pretending the video itself is clickable.
5. **Restraint protects trust.** Red remains controlled failure, green verified pass, amber uncertainty, and cyan structure. No effect may obscure a claim, citation, scope label, screenshot, or karaoke caption.

## Signature “wow” moments

- **S01:** human-stakes type assembles in 3D; one request hits an energy ring and physically fractures into two colored paths; consequence labels land after the split.
- **S06:** genuine FlowGuard UI opens through a light aperture, rotates into a premium browser stage, receives an on-screen `PROVE IT` cursor click, then resolves into Analyzer → Controlled Lab value architecture.
- **S08:** fail and pass are visually opposed worlds. Red pulses collide into two effects; a cyan reset wipe changes the energy field; green requests converge through `RESERVE KEY`; genuine before/after evidence fills the stage.
- **S09–S10:** historic and final evidence are separated as an editorial chronology, then counters assemble into an auditable post-merge verification wall.
- **S11:** evidence/before/verdicts kinetic type collapses into the FlowGuard mark while three proof orbits lock around the brand.

## Compositing stack

- Layered radial and conic light fields with slow parallax.
- Perspective camera motion and temporary screenshot magnification.
- Anamorphic light wipes, impact flashes, pulse rings, particles, and controlled chromatic separation.
- Glass materials with moving reflective sheens.
- Fine scan texture, film grain, HUD corners, deterministic timecode, and master progress telemetry.
- Word-synchronized karaoke captions isolated from hero typography and evidence labels.

## Quality gates

- No unchanged hero composition longer than seven seconds.
- No browser screenshot reconstructed or content-altered.
- No unsupported claim introduced for visual drama.
- Every effect must preserve caption/evidence readability at 1080p.
- Rendered proof frames—not source inspection—decide acceptance.
- Expensive blur effects are rejected if they make the 3:30 film operationally impractical to reproduce.

## Current proof result

- S01 was rendered at native 1920×1080 and inspected at four frames per second.
- Six native 1920×1080 S08 states were rendered and inspected: vulnerable flow, verified fail evidence, corrected flow, verified pass evidence, A/B compare, and final result.
- The optimized gradient/glow stack preserves the premium look while avoiding the initial high-cost volumetric blur implementation.
