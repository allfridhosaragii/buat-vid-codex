# FlowGuard V3 — Voice and Timing Provenance

## Generation

- Engine: `kokoro-onnx` 0.6.1, CPU inference.
- Model license: Apache-2.0.
- Voice: stock `af_heart`; no voice cloning, uploaded speaker sample, paid API, or private model was used.
- Model SHA-256: `beb0d1848dee9a49da392cc3df26958d46cfa35d321edf434f52949153f0df3a`.
- Voices SHA-256: `bca610b8308e8d99f32e6fe4197e7ec01679264efed0cac9140fe9c29f1fbf7d`.
- Eleven scene profiles vary pace and punctuation pauses while retaining one coherent narrator identity. Profiles range from reflective S01/S11 to energized S08 and crisp S10.

## Auditions and selection

- Generated 30 phrase auditions: 10 representative lines at restrained (0.92×), natural (1.00×), and energized (1.08×) delivery.
- Selected per-scene profiles by narrative role, duration fit, continuity, and phoneme-alignment results.
- The cloud environment has no reliable human listening path. The auditions were generated and measured, but subjective acoustic listening approval is intentionally not claimed.
- Chatterbox was not installed because the accepted CPU-capable Kokoro route produced legal, deterministic narration and genuine model-derived timing without adding a second large model or uncertain compute cost.

## Word synchronization

- `src/data/word-timings.json` is derived from the generated speech model's contextual phoneme durations.
- Each narration word is phonemized in context and aligned to the returned model phoneme sequence with Needleman–Wunsch alignment; timestamps are not divided evenly across scene duration.
- Scene alignment confidence ranges from 93.72% to 100%. Automated QA rejects missing audio, non-monotonic words, words past audio duration, scene overflow, or confidence below 90%.

## Original sound design

- `MASTER_SFX.wav` and `PILOT_SFX.wav` are generated by the repository's deterministic Python synthesizer.
- No third-party samples or commercial music are embedded.
- The master contains 47 shot-synchronized tonal events, nine transition sweeps, and scene-specific energy levels beneath narration.
